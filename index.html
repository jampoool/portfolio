01 · About

I care about what happens after "it works."

I'm Jan Paul Daguman, a Front-End / Full-Stack Web Developer from the Philippines with a background in Information Technology. I work across the full stack — from the interface a visitor clicks on, to the Laravel or WordPress backend that keeps the site running.

My approach is simple: a website should look considered, load fast, and be easy for the client to maintain long after the project ships. I spend as much attention on spacing, hierarchy, and responsiveness as I do on the code that powers a checkout flow or a REST API.

I help clients turn a rough idea, a Figma file, or an existing outdated site into something they're proud to send people to — whether that's a marketing website, a WooCommerce store, or a custom internal tool.
